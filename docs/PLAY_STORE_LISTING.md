## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
NfcEmu turns your Android phone into a programmable NFC tag. Instead of
carrying physical NFC stickers, emulate one on your phone and tap it against
any NFC reader or NFC-capable device - just like a real tag, but instantly
editable and completely offline.

WHAT YOU CAN EMULATE
• Websites - open any URL on tap
• Phone numbers - start a call
• Email - open a pre-filled message
• SMS - open a pre-filled text message
• Locations - open a map at a set of coordinates
• Play Store links - open an app's store page
• Wi-Fi credentials - join a network on tap
• Business cards (vCard) - share full contact details
• Plain text
• Any custom URI

MULTIPLE PROFILES, SWITCHED INSTANTLY
Save as many profiles as you like, pin your favorites, and switch the active
one from the home screen, the profile list, a home screen widget, or a
Quick Settings tile - no need to open the app every time.

SCAN PHYSICAL TAGS TOO
Already have NFC tags lying around? Scan one to read its content into a
pre-filled, editable profile instead of typing everything by hand.

BACK UP AND SHARE YOUR PROFILES
Export any profile as a re-editable file, or export your whole library at
once as a single archive. Import them back in on this device or another one.

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
NfcEmu actively suppresses the phone's own tag-reading behavior while it's
open, so holding two NFC phones together doesn't cause confusing "empty tag"
prompts on either side.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
NfcEmu works entirely offline. There's no analytics, no ads, no account, and
no network permission at all - the app is physically incapable of sending
your data anywhere. Everything you create stays on your device unless you
explicitly export or share it yourself.
```
